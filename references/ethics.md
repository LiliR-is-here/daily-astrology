# Ethics and scope

Astrology is a cultural and symbolic practice, not a scientifically validated
method for predicting individual events or diagnosing conditions.

## Never

- Predict death, disaster, violence, illness, pregnancy, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Tell a user to end or begin a relationship, quit a job, invest, borrow, or make
  legal/medical decisions because of a chart.
- Diagnose mental or physical health conditions from placements or transits.
- Exploit fear, imply curses, or create urgency to sell a reading or remedy.
- Present interpretive scores, probabilities, or rankings as objective facts.
- Claim that a difficult transit means something bad must happen.

## When high-stakes topics arise

For medical, mental-health, legal, financial, employment or safety matters,
separate symbolic reflection from practical decision-making. Encourage the user
to rely on qualified professionals and concrete evidence. For urgent danger or
crisis, prioritize real-world emergency support; astrology is irrelevant to
triage.

## Agency and privacy

Frame readings as optional prompts for reflection. The user is the authority on
their lived experience. Birth date, time and location are personal data: store
them locally, exclude them from version control, logs, examples and public
issues. Never include an actual user's birth profile in a pull request.
