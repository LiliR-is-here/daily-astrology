# Transit interpretation framework

## 1. Separate calculation from meaning

Astronomical calculation establishes positions, angular relationships and
timestamps. Astrological meanings are symbolic conventions. Never present the
interpretation as an empirically established causal mechanism.

## 2. Rank factors

Use a qualitative three-tier selection, not a numeric score:

- **Primary:** close transit-to-natal aspects relevant to the user's question;
  repeated activation of the same natal planet/point; reliable contacts to
  personal planets or angles.
- **Secondary:** meaningful but wider aspects, relevant house placements when
  birth time and house system are reliable, and multiple factors reinforcing a
  common theme.
- **Context:** sign ingresses, lunations, general planetary weather, and
  generational transits without a close personal contact.

Within a tier, prefer tighter orbs, applying contacts where timing is supplied,
repetition, and relevance to the user's stated concern. Do not assume that
applying always means stronger in every interpretive tradition.

## 3. Duration matters

- Moon: hours to a couple of days; use as a passing mood or timing accent.
- Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars: days to weeks, depending on speed and retrograde.
- Jupiter and Saturn: months or longer; describe as background themes.
- Uranus, Neptune, Pluto: long cycles; require a close natal contact or clear
  relevance before foregrounding them.

These are broad interpretive ranges, not calculated end dates. Use exact
engine-provided timing if available.

## 4. Synthesize, do not catalogue

Combine multiple indicators into one coherent possibility. For example, several
verified contacts involving Saturn and the Moon may be discussed as a period
where emotional needs and obligations feel more visible. Do not claim that
this guarantees conflict, sadness, rejection, or a particular event.

A useful synthesis contains:
- the shared theme;
- the tension or trade-off;
- what the user may notice in ordinary life;
- a choice or experiment that preserves agency.

## 5. Translate into everyday language

Prefer:
- “You may notice…”
- “This is a useful day to pay attention to…”
- “If this theme is present, try…”
- “Check whether this actually matches your experience.”

Avoid:
- “The universe will make you…”
- “You are destined to…”
- “This transit guarantees…”
- diagnosis, relationship verdicts, or financial predictions.

## 6. Avoid false precision

Do not invent luck scores, percentages, rankings presented as objective truth,
lucky colors/numbers, or event probabilities. A qualitative priority label is
only a reading aid, not a measurement of real-world impact.
