# Daily report format

Use this structure. Keep it compact; omit sections that lack supporting data.

## 今日的天象

State the date and timezone used, calculation backend, zodiac and house system
when available. Mention only the strongest verified placements/aspects.

## 今日主线

One sentence naming the dominant theme, followed by a short explanation grounded
in one to three calculated factors.

## 可能映射到的生活场景

Give up to three concrete, ordinary-life examples. Phrase them as possibilities,
not predictions. Do not force every life domain into the reading.

## 可以留意的张力

Describe the trade-off or tendency that may be worth observing. Avoid fear,
fatalism and overgeneralization.

## 一个小行动

Offer one low-burden, controllable action. It should remain useful even if the
astrological interpretation does not resonate.

## 今日观察问题

Ask one specific question that helps the user compare the reading with lived
experience, rather than searching for proof that it is true.

## 数据与限制

Briefly disclose unknown birth time, uncertain angles/houses, unsupported house
system, missing fields, or calculation fallback. Do not bury important limits.
